Behold Toad Cane, a modular attachment that can be added to any cane to allow the user to pick up items without having to bend over. The design was made as lightweight and simple as possible from laser-cut acrylic, with minimal electronics and no microcontrollers. It worked well for regular items like water bottles and (super critically) socks.
