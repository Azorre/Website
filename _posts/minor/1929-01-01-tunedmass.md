Throughout college I had a habit of making things a lot more difficult for myself than they needed to be. This was a classic example. The assignment was to collect vibration data on something, but I took it on myself to a tunable mass absorber (not a damper, because adding damping was where the line was drawn on complexity) and a building prototype to house said absorber.

I welded up a basic steel frame with diagonal bracing for added rigidity. I then designed and printed a compound pendulum where the weight could be moved to change the natural frequency. We then used an impact hammer to determine the response at different pendulum positions.
