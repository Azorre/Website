I made two mechantronics robots in college. The first was The Dinosaur Lightning Tornado, a battlebot style robot that used IR sensors to stay within a ring and push opponents out. The robot is made of lasercut and 3D printed parts, and we even cast our own wheels from silicone. The micro-controller is an ATmega328P with assembly code. 
![Silicone wheels in mold](assets/images/mechatronics/wheel1.jpeg "Silicone wheels in 3DP mold")
![Silicone wheels](assets/images/mechatronics/wheel2.jpeg "Silicone wheels final product")
Grip is super important for the competiton, so I designed some wheels with a 3D printed core with super grippy silicone cast around it. I made a 3D printed mold for the silicone pour that could be easily cut off afterwards. I also lasercut a hub out of 1/4in steel.
![Autonomous mobile robot](assets/images/mechatronics/amr1.png "Autonomous mobile robot")
I also built a robot that could navigate an obstacle course using ultrasonic sensors and fire a ping-pong ball at at an enemy robot or base, which it would detect using an IR sensor. It had mecanum wheels, meaning it could translate laterally or diagonally, as well as just rolling normally.
