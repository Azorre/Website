This was my first ever robotics project. I made a remote controlled, Arduino-based robot that could pick up and deliver a bottle cap from one cup into another. I laser-cut and 3D printed the frame, including a rack and pinion attachment at the front for manipulating the bottle cap. It worked pretty well in competition.
