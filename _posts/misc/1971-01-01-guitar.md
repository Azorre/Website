Guitars are really simple when it comes to woodworking or electronics, but demand a lot of finesse which makes them a lot of fun to work on. This mini guitar came disassembled and unfinished from a kit. This was the priming and painting.

![Priming the guitar](/assets/images/misc/guitar/primer1.jpg)   ![Priming the guitar](/assets/images/misc/guitar/silver1.jpg) 

The silver paint was not as vibrant as I had hoped, so I ended up stripping it down and staining it blue instead. I also stained the fretboard with some Danish Oil.
