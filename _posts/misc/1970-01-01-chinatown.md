New York City is spending close to $4 Billion to build a skyscraper jail in the historic and culturally rich neighborhood of Chinatown. It is also being built without any consultation with the local community, and in a way that physically damages nearby buildings and quality of life in the neighborhood. This is an opiniated video that explored the context and reality of the plan to replace Riker's island (partially) with a jail in Manhattan Chinatown.
