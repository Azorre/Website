Skateboards are my primary form of transport in New York City, so I've had a great time personalizing them over the years (and for friends!):
![Skateboard Lasercut!](/assets/images/misc/skate/laser1.gif)
![Wavy Skateboard](/assets/images/misc/skate/wave1.jpg)   ![Pigeon Skateboard](/assets/images/misc/skate/pigeon1.jpg)

This was a gift for a friend who REALLY likes Astroboy:
![Astroboy Griptape](/assets/images/misc/skate/astro1.jpg)    ![Astroboy paint](/assets/images/misc/skate/astro2.jpg)

At some point I welded together a skateboard out of steel tubes I had laying around. I bent the curves by hand and laser-cut some hangers for the trucks to bolt into. It was surprisingly functional. I even landed an ollie at some point, though it was very loud if you missed.
