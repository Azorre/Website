The 4 years I spent working at my school's makerspace was singlehandedly the most fulfilling thing I did there. I started out without any real knowledge of digital design or fabrication, but ended up learning how to use 3D printers, laser-cutters, CNC routers, waterjet machines, 3D scanners, and even a 6-axis robotic arm. 

I used all these tools extensively for my own projects, but I got way more experience from helping hundreds of artists, architects, and engineers with all these machines. I got to see these machines used and abused in every possible way, and to troubleshoot every issue that could possibly arise.
