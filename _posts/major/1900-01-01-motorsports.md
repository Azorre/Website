I joined my school's electric racecar team as soon as I joined college. I became aerodynamics and frame lead, and eventually Chief Mechanical Engineer for the 25 person team. After two long years, we finally took the racecar to Michigan to race against other teams. I wish I could say it went well, but instead I'll say I learned a lot from my time with the team.

# Chief Engineer things
![The team!](/assets/images/major/motorsports/team2.jpg "The team!")
I became Chief Mechanical Engineer of the team in my sophomore year, and oversaw the design and manufacture of all the mechanical components of the car, including frame, suspension components, powertrain, and more. Part of the duties included training and organizing members of the team, coordinating with faculty and outside sponsors, and managing finances.

# Aerodynamics
![Picture of an aerodynamic simulation](/assets/images/major/motorsports/streamlines.png "CFD of the car")
In my first semester of college I began learning basic Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulations (while I was also learning CAD for the first time) and combined that with my rudimentary understanding of aerodynamics to design and test an aerodynamic package for the car. We ended up not using the wings in competition, but I definitely learned a lot in the process.

![Carbon fiber layup!](/assets/images/san-juan-mountains.jpg "Carbon fiber layup!")
I also most of the carbon fiber bodywork and wings (that we never used) for the car. We used wet layups and vacuum bags for most parts. 

![Resin infusion layup](/assets/images/major/motorsports/infusion1.jpg "Resin infusion layup")
We also did a resin infusion layup for the nosecone!
![Resin infusion layup](/assets/images/major/motorsports/nosecone1.jpg "Resin infusion layup")




# Frame
![Picture of an FEA simulation](/assets/images/major/motorsports/fea1.png "Bendy car")
I taught myself basic Finite Element Analysis (FEA) in order to simulate the torsional rigidity of the steel spaceframe chassis. I then validated those results using a real life torsion rig on the physical frame. There was around 10% error.
![Torsion Rig](/assets/images/major/motorsports/torsion1.jpg "Torsion Rig")
